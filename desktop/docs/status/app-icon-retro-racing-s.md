# app-icon-retro-racing-s: a fourth app icon, Retro Racing S

## The change

Settings ▸ General ▸ App icon offers a fourth icon, **Retro Racing S**
(`desktop.app_icon: retro-racing-s`), last after Racing S: Racing S's raster
first draft, twin white enamel stripes forming an S on a glossy cobalt tile.
It is the `App Icon / Racing S` component (`2366:9180`) from the Figma frame
`Shelbi · Desktop app icon · Three directions` (`2362:40717`), the frame
Retro (`2366:9174`) comes from.

The four names need about 61 columns of the settings pane. Narrower, the row
falls back as before: only the chosen icon, and a click (or Enter or →)
steps to the next.

## How it works

- The source is a single flattened raster (generated artwork in the macOS
  mask, with its framing and shadow), not layers, so it ships as Retro does:
  `assets/icon/retro-racing-s-1024.png` is the component's own 1024px export,
  and `scripts/app-icon.sh` cuts `retro-racing-s-{512,224,128}.png` from it
  with `sips`. The same export of Retro's component reproduces
  `retro-1024.png` pixel for pixel, and `sips` reproduces Retro's committed
  cuts byte for byte.
- `shelbi_state::AppIcon::RetroRacingS` (`retro-racing-s`, label "Retro
  Racing S"), last in `AppIcon::ALL`; `lenient_app_icon` reads
  `retro-racing-s`, `retro_racing_s`, `Retro-Racing-S`.
- The desktop: `app_icon.rs` (About and tile images), `platform/dock_icon.rs`
  (the Dock's 512px picture). The bundle keeps wearing Command Grille.
- The TUI: `about_icon::RETRO_RACING_S` (generated by
  `scripts/tui-about-icon.py`) for About; the App icon row needed no change
  beyond the new `AppIcon`, since it iterates `AppIcon::ALL`.
